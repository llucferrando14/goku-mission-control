# Ventures Skill

## Overview

**Domain:** Market Intelligence & Opportunity Scouting  
**Purpose:** Find strategic opportunities matching Lluc's rare skill combination  
**Output:** Actionable briefs on tools, trends, and gaps  

---

## What I Monitor

### Tools & Technology
- New AI tools launching
- Developer productivity tools
- Growth/marketing automation
- Health/athletic tech

### Market Trends
- AI agent ecosystem evolution
- Growth engineering best practices
- Mobile game market shifts
- Consulting/Growth Partner demand

### Strategic Gaps
- Unserved niches
- Tool combinations nobody's built
- Skills in high demand, low supply
- Emerging markets

---

## Opportunity Criteria

### High Priority
- **Directly relevant** to active projects
- **Time-sensitive** (limited beta, early pricing)
- **High ROI** (big impact for low effort)
- **Rare skill match** (fits Lluc's unique combo)

### Evaluation Framework
1. **Relevance:** How directly applicable?
2. **Timing:** Urgent or can wait?
3. **ROI:** Effort vs. potential gain?
4. **Uniqueness:** Does it leverage Lluc's rare skills?
5. **Feasibility:** Can Lluc actually execute?

---

## Output Types

### Opportunity Brief
Quick analysis of a spotted opportunity:
- What it is
- Why it matters
- Recommended action

### Investment Memo
Deeper analysis for significant opportunities:
- Market analysis
- Competitive landscape
- Strategic recommendation
- Implementation plan

### MVP Spec
If building something new:
- Core features
- Technical architecture
- Launch plan
